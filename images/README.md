# Images

This folder contains visualization screenshots generated during the Shopping Trends Analysis project.

## Files

- `age_distribution.png` - Histogram showing the distribution of customer ages.
- `gender_distribution.png` - Pie chart showing male and female customer distribution.
- `item_purchased_countplot.png` - Count plot showing the frequency of different items purchased.
- `category_distribution.png` - Pie chart showing product category distribution.
- `season_distribution.png` - Pie chart showing season-wise purchase distribution.
- `gender_purchase_amount.png` - Bar chart comparing total purchase amount by gender.
- `season_category_analysis.png` - Grouped bar chart showing product category purchases across seasons.
